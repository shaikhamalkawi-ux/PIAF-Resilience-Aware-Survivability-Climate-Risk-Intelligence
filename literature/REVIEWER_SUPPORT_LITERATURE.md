# Reviewer-support literature and data map

This file records sources added or verified for ECMX-D-26-01378 major revision. Sources are used only where they directly support a reviewer-requested change; they do not numerically calibrate the declared technology scenario scores.

## R3-Major-2 — Almasad et al. (2023)
**Site suitability analysis for implementing solar PV power plants using GIS and fuzzy MCDM based approach**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.solener.2022.11.046
Use: Solar irradiance/resource magnitude is an explicit site-screening criterion; supports keeping resource potential separate and visible.
Access: Publisher page; article metadata publicly accessible

## R3-Major-2 — Noorollahi et al. (2022)
**A framework for GIS-based site selection and technical potential evaluation of PV solar farm using Fuzzy-Boolean logic and AHP multi-criteria decision-making approach**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.renene.2021.12.124
Use: Separates site suitability from technical PV potential and explicitly uses annual horizontal irradiation.
Access: Publisher/metadata page

## R3-Major-4/6; R4 — Ranjgar et al. (2026)
**Solar PV site selection using GIS-MCDM: A comprehensive approach with spatially explicit collinearity and sensitivity analyses**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.renene.2026.125873
Use: Recent PV siting study emphasizing criterion interdependence and sensitivity; useful for discussion and robustness comparison.
Access: Open access / CC BY

## R3-Minor-6 — Habte et al. (2020)
**Long-term spatial and temporal solar resource variability over America using the NSRDB version 3 (1998–2017)**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.rser.2020.110285
Use: Shows coefficient of variation is used for GHI variability and that seasonal variability differs strongly by season; supports careful interpretation of raw CV.
Access: Publisher/metadata page

## R3-Minor-6 — Voyant et al. (2026)
**Stochastic coefficient of variation: Assessing the variability and forecastability of solar irradiance**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.renene.2025.123913
Use: Shows conventional variability metrics do not isolate stochastic fluctuations from deterministic trends and proposes a bounded alternative; supports limitation/future-work statement.
Access: Open access / CC license

## R3-Minor-5; R3-Major-3 — Nan et al. (2024)
**Investigation on temperature dependence of recent high-efficiency silicon solar modules**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.solmat.2023.112649
Use: Directly compares temperature dependence of PERC, SHJ and TOPCon; supports the thermal component and technology-specific caution.
Access: Publisher page

## R3-Major-3/8; R4 — Adothu et al. (2024)
**Comprehensive review on performance, reliability, and roadmap of c-Si PV modules in desert climates: A proposal for improved testing standard**
DOI/URL: 10.1002/pip.3827
Use: Desert PV review linking high irradiance with harsh thermal/UV/soiling stress and emphasizing climate-specific testing.
Access: Wiley article page

## R3-Major-3/8; R4 — Abdallah et al. (2023)
**Performance and reliability of crystalline-silicon photovoltaics in desert climate**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.solener.2022.11.042
Use: Five-year desert field evidence showing product/technology-specific degradation and failure modes.
Access: Open access / CC BY

## R3-Major-3/8; R4 — IEA PVPS Task 13 (2025)
**Optimisation of Photovoltaic Systems for Different Climates**
DOI/URL: 10.69766/qsyc8858
Use: Authoritative climate-specific PV reliability/performance guidance; supports climate-aware but non-predictive framing.
Access: Official open report

**Solar Flux Assessment / Validation**

## R3-Major-6 — Mazurek and Strzalka (2022)
**On the Monte Carlo weights in multiple criteria decision analysis**
DOI/URL: 10.1371/journal.pone.0268950
Use: Provides a defensible example of Monte Carlo over feasible weight space; used to explain why the revised paper avoids unsupported probability distributions and instead uses structural sensitivity.
Access: Open access PLOS ONE

## R3-Major-3/8; R4 — Kivambe et al. (2025)
**Comprehensive assessment of performance and reliability of PERC, TOPCon and SHJ modules in desert climates**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.solener.2025.113555
Use: Three-year Qatar field evidence shows strong product/BOM heterogeneity within modern PV families and supports conditional rather than universal technology conclusions.
Access: Publisher page; open-access article.

## R3-Major-3/6; R4 — Sen et al. (2024)
**Buyer aware: Three new failure modes in TOPCon modules absent from PERC technology**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.solmat.2024.112877
Use: Damp-heat tests show strong bill-of-materials dependence in TOPCon response; supports treating family-level resilience descriptors as scenario assumptions rather than fixed empirical constants.
Access: Open access / CC BY.

## R3-Major-3/8; R4 — Mehdi et al. (2023)
**An experimental comparative analysis of different PV technologies performance including the influence of hot-arid climatic parameters: Toward a realistic yield assessment for desert locations**
DOI/URL: 10.1016/j.renene.2023.01.082
Use: Hot-arid field comparison of crystalline-silicon and CdTe explicitly considers temperature and soiling; supports technology-specific climate-response discussion.
Access: Publisher page.
