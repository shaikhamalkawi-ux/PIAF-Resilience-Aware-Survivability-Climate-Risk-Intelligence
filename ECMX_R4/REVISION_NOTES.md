# ECMX-R4 editorial closure notes

R4 follows an independent editorial audit of ECMX-R3. It does not restore the removed MCDM branch, market layer, cross-system technology ranking, or uncalibrated technology-family coefficients.

## Scientific closures

1. **Climate-component weights**: equal weights are now described as a baseline convention, not as a neutral or calibrated physical weighting. A deterministic one-component weight-path audit varies each component from 0 to 1 while the other three share the remainder equally. The baseline two-site Pareto set is most locally sensitive to humidity weighting: it is retained up to a humidity weight of about 0.2952 on this path; above that threshold Aswan alone is nondominated.
2. **Temporal robustness**: Aswan and Antofagasta are individually nondominated in all 10,000 year-block bootstrap resamples, but the exact two-site set occurs in 9,983/10,000 resamples (99.83%); Alice Springs joins them in 17 resamples.
3. **Normalization**: component normalization is benchmark-relative. The composite is the arithmetic mean of normalized components and is not forced to attain 0 or 1. `C=0` would require one location to attain the benchmark minimum in all four components.
4. **Wind interpretation**: daily high wind is retained as an external-exposure descriptor, not a gust-load or one-directional physical-damage coefficient. Possible convective cooling is acknowledged; no thermal wind model is added.
5. **Aggregation boundary**: if resource and all four climate descriptors are treated as five separate objectives, all ten locations are nondominated. Shortlist reduction therefore depends on the declared climate-summary aggregation convention; R4 states this explicitly.
